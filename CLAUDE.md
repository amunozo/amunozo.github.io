## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, research leads, and academic contacts visiting a personal website during a job search. They need to understand quickly who Alberto is, what kind of roles he wants, and what kind of work he does.

### Brand Personality
Refined, elegant, beautiful. Artistic rather than corporate. Serious enough to feel credible, but not stiff, generic, startup-like, or purely academic.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light mode only. Refined and artistic with a bit of complexity, but not overloaded. Color is welcome, but it should feel curated and intentional rather than loud or trendy. Avoid startup landing page patterns and avoid the default academic personal-site look.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with typography, composition, and rhythm before decorative effects.
2. Use color sparingly but confidently, with a small curated palette rather than many arbitrary accents.
3. Prefer asymmetry, open space, and section structure over cards and boxed layouts.
4. Keep the homepage expressive and memorable, but still easy to scan for recruiters.
5. Make the site feel designed by a person with taste, not generated from a generic portfolio template.
