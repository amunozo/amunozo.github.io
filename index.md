<p>My work focuses on <strong>efficient, robust, and interpretable language models</strong>, especially in <strong>multilingual, low-resource, and non-standard language settings</strong>. I combine research depth with hands-on experience building and evaluating models end to end.</p>

<div class="focus-block">
  <p class="focus-label">I have worked on</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>structured NLP</strong>, including nested named entity recognition</li>
    <li><strong>multilingual and non-standard language modeling</strong>, including transfer across language varieties with pixel-based models</li>
    <li><strong>evaluation and analysis of LLMs and autoregressive transformers</strong></li>
    <li><strong>reproducible experimental pipelines</strong> with <strong>Python, PyTorch, Hugging Face, Docker, and GPU workflows</strong></li>
  </ul>
</div>

<p>I was also a visiting researcher at the <a href="https://mainlp.github.io/">MaiNLP Research Lab</a> at <a href="https://www.lmu.de/en/">LMU Munich</a> and the <a href="https://nlp.epfl.ch/">NLP Lab</a> at <a href="https://www.epfl.ch/schools/ic/">EPFL Lausanne</a>.</p>

<p>I have first-authored papers at <strong>ACL, EMNLP, COLING, IJCNLP-AACL, and RANLP</strong>.</p>
